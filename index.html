About us

A fabrication shop first.
A contractor by discipline.

Oteh Miroko Aluminum started in 2009 as a two-man workshop cutting aluminium sections for Abakaliki homeowners. Sixteen years later we run a dedicated 1,400 m² fabrication facility in Abakaliki, a small installation fleet and a glazing crew — and we still measure every opening ourselves.

That matters more than it sounds. Windows and glass fail at the detail: a sloping reveal, a lintel that isn't level, a drainage slot facing the wrong way. We survey on site, fabricate to those true dimensions, and install with the same crew that built the units — so accountability never gets subcontracted.

  • Survey → shop drawing → approval before a single cut is made.
  • Powder-coated, anodised or timber-look finishes matched to your façade.
  • Compliant glazing: toughened, laminated or double-glazed units with certification.
  • Post-install service for hardware adjustment, resealing and replacement panels.

How we work

Five steps, no surprises

A fixed sequence that protects both the finish quality and your programme.

  1. 01

    Site survey & measurement

    We measure every opening, check levels, plumb and fixing substrates, and note access constraints for the install crew.

  2. 02

    Shop drawings & quote

    You receive elevations, profile sections, glass specification and a line-item quotation — no lump-sum guesswork.

  3. 03

    Fabrication

    Cutting, crimping, welding, powder-coat matching and glazing happen on our floor under one quality check sheet.

  4. 04

    Installation

    Our own installers set, pack, fix, seal and clean down. Programme updates sent to you as each zone is completed.

  5. 05

    Handover & aftercare

    Care guide, warranty documents and a scheduled callback to adjust hardware after the first wet season.
