Budgeting

What actually drives the cost of aluminium windows

A useful comparison starts with the complete specification, not just the price per opening.

24 May 2026 · Oteh Miroko Aluminum

Two window quotations can look very different because they may describe different products or different amounts of work. Before comparing totals, check that the opening schedule, glass, hardware, finish and installation scope match.

The main specification factors

  • Profile system. Series, section sizes and opening style affect the amount of material and the way each window operates.
  • Glass. Type, thickness, safety requirements, coatings and pane dimensions all form part of the glass specification.
  • Hardware. Handles, rollers, locks and restrictors should suit the window type, expected use and exposure.
  • Finish. The selected colour and finish can affect preparation, coating and lead time. Ask for the finish to be identified clearly.
  • Quantity and repetition. A repeated schedule can be planned differently from many unique sizes. Accurate opening counts and measurements help avoid allowances based on guesswork.
  • Access and installation. Floor level, site access, removal of existing frames, scaffolding and working conditions may change the installation scope.

How to compare quotations

Ask each fabricator to itemise the system, glass, finish, hardware, fabrication, delivery and installation. Compare the same opening schedule and confirm what is excluded, such as making good, access equipment or disposal of old frames.

Provide drawings or a clear list of opening sizes, the project location, floor levels and preferred finishes. If measurements are not final, label estimates as provisional and arrange a site measurement before approving production.

Make the decision on value, not only the headline

A lower initial price may reflect a different specification or scope. Check warranty terms, installation responsibilities and the stated materials before deciding. A detailed quotation makes it easier to ask focused questions and choose an option that matches the project.
