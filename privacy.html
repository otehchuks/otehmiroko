Privacy notice

This is a working draft, not an approved company policy. Confirm the highlighted business details and have the notice reviewed before publishing.

Draft dated 7 October 2026

Information this site may collect

If you submit a quotation request, the form may collect your name, phone number, email address, project location, requested service, budget range, project details and any files you attach. The site host may also process routine technical data needed to deliver and secure the website.

How information may be used

Enquiry details are intended to help Oteh Miroko Aluminum respond to your request, discuss project requirements, arrange measurement or follow-up, and protect the site from spam or misuse. Confirm the company's final purposes and any applicable legal basis before this draft is adopted.

Service providers and external services

The quotation form is configured for Netlify Forms when this site is hosted on Netlify. Google Fonts are requested from Google to display the selected typefaces. The map is loaded from Google only after a visitor chooses the interactive-map control. These providers may receive technical information such as an IP address when their services are used. Confirm the current provider list, settings and any international data transfers before publishing this notice.

Retention and security

Business owner: insert the approved retention periods for enquiries and uploaded files. The company should also document its access controls, deletion process and any retention required for contracts or legal obligations. No website can promise absolute security, but appropriate safeguards should be used for the systems that store enquiry data.

Your choices and requests

Subject to applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or raise a concern about its handling. To make a request, contact hello@otehmirokoaluminum.com. Confirm the correct contact address and the company's process for handling these requests before publication.

Cookies and third-party content

This draft assumes no analytics or advertising scripts are enabled. External fonts and the map service are third-party content; the map loads only after you request it. Recheck this section if analytics, advertising, embedded services or additional cookies are added.

Details to approve before publication

  • Confirm the responsible legal entity and privacy contact.
  • Set retention periods for enquiries, attachments and server logs.
  • Confirm Netlify, email and any other data processors actually used.
  • Have the final notice reviewed for the company's operations and applicable Nigerian data-protection requirements.
